About Me

I'm Orlando Santiago III. I've spent my career in network engineering and security, working with firewalls, load balancers, and the infrastructure that keeps applications online. Today I lead a team of channel solutions engineers at F5.

I've spent years working on the back end of the network, and WDD 130 is where I'm learning the front end. This portal brings that work together, and I plan to keep growing it into a portfolio long after the class ends.
